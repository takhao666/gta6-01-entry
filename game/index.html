STREETS OF GOLD

Solmar City

You are Jax Marlow, a driver for hire in a sun-soaked coastal city run by Viktor Hale. His fixer Lena Cross has six jobs for you — if you can stay ahead of the Solmar PD. Steal cars, run missions, build your name.

An original open-world crime game. No network needed — everything runs from these files.

HOW TO PLAY

W A S D / Arrows — walk or drive  ·  Shift — sprint / handbrake
E — get in / out of a car, use a shop  ·  Mouse — aim, Click — shoot
F / Space — punch  ·  1 / 2 — fists / pistol
L — start the next mission  ·  P / Esc — pause  ·  M — mute
G Gun Shop · S Pay & Spray · H Hospital · $ Safehouse (save)

Steal cars, but the law notices. Wanted stars bring cruisers and foot officers — break line of sight to cool down, or pay for a new paint job. Missions pay cash; cash buys guns and ammo. Get wasted or busted and you lose money.
